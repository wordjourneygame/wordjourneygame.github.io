Block League: Block Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Block League: Block Puzzle app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The app is published on Google Play by StrongWoman.

1. Information we collect

Guest account. When you first open the app, an anonymous guest account is created automatically. It has a random player ID and no personal details.

Information you choose to provide. A nickname, your country, and optionally your region (province/state) and district. These are shown publicly next to your scores on leaderboards. Do not use your real full name as a nickname.

Google sign-in (optional). If you link a Google account, we receive your Google account email address and a Google account identifier so your progress can be saved and restored. Your email is never shown to other players.

Game data. Scores, games played, lines cleared, daily missions, achievements, district founding activity and titles (such as Mayor / Deputy Mayor), and whether a "second chance" was used. This is needed to verify scores and run leaderboards.

App activity and diagnostics. We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how the app is used, for example which screens are opened and when a game starts or ends. This includes device information and app instance identifiers.

Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location) and ad interaction data. The app does not request location permission.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and show leaderboards and district boards.
  • To prevent cheating and verify scores on our servers.
  • To improve the app using aggregated analytics.
  • To show ads, which keep the app free.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase – stores player accounts, nicknames, locations you selected and game data.
  • Google Firebase – Google sign-in and analytics.
  • Google AdMob – advertising and the consent form.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads or analytics are used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads.

5. Invites

When you invite friends to found a district, the app creates a share link that contains your district and nickname. You choose who to send it to.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your player, scores and progress at any time in Settings → Delete my data. You can also request deletion by email (see below); we will respond within 30 days. Aggregated, anonymous statistics may be kept.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Computer-controlled players

While the community grows, World and Country leaderboards may include computer-controlled players. They are not real people and no personal data is associated with them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
